# Darul Attar - Product Context

## Identity & Mission
Darul Attar is a boutique artisanal perfumery in Thousand Lights, Greams Road, Chennai (Tamil Nadu, India). The house specializes in 100% alcohol-free concentrated perfume oils (attars), pure Assam & Cambodian Oudhs, Ruh Khus (Vetiver), Taif Rose, and traditional Arabian amber & musk compositions.

## Target Audience
- Connoisseurs of authentic non-alcoholic oriental fragrance oils seeking skin-safe, long-lasting natural perfumes for daily wear and prayer.
- Gifting and bridal fragrance buyers in Chennai and across India requiring authentic oud oils and artisanal presentation.
- Patrons seeking traditional heritage distillation without harsh synthetic additives.

## Core Value Proposition
- 100% pure alcohol-free concentrated oil formulations (attar / ittar).
- Direct provenance: Pure Assam oudh, Kannauj hydro-distilled Ruh Khus, Taif rose, ambergris-style musk blends.
- Physical presence in Thousand Lights, Chennai with pan-India expedited dispatch via Delhivery.

## Aesthetic Constraints
- Color palette: Warm Champagne Gold (`#C0A080`), Burnished Ochre (`#8C6D47`), Soft Alabaster (`#EFE5DA`), Crisp Canvas (`#FFFFFF`), Warm Alabaster (`#FAF9F6`), Deep Charcoal (`#111827`).
- Tone: Dignified, understated luxury, artisanal heritage, grounded, calm.
- Strictly prohibited: Any divinity-adjacent or absolute superlatives ("perfect"), shirk/kufr phrasing, figurative human/animal faces or depictions.
